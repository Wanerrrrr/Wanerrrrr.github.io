Baking & Texturing

This work is based on the original concept Xuanwu by artist Hua Lu , which reimagines the ancient Chinese deity Xuanwu, also known as the Black Tortoise. Xuanwu is often depicted as a combination of a tortoise and a serpent, symbolizing protection, longevity, and the balance of natural forces. For this piece, I used a PBR workflow to model and texture the character, closely following the original artwork for reference. I began by sculpting the base geometry and refining the shapes to capture the combination of reptilian armor and skeletal structure. The texture work was done using substance painter to bring out the details, all while ensuring a natural and realistic look. I paid special attention to the materials and lighting to create a balanced and visually striking final render. This work was created as part of the “Veiled Realms” challenge , where I aimed to explore a mythical creature existing between realms, combining both organic and mystical elements to convey the essence of Xuanwu as a divine protector.
